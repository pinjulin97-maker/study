# Physics HL

IB syllabus (first assessment 2025), five themes:

- **A** — [Space, time and motion](A-space-time-and-motion/) (kinematics, forces, momentum, work & energy, rigid-body rotation, relativity)
- **B** — [The particulate nature of matter](B-particulate-nature-of-matter/) (thermal physics, gases, first & second laws of thermodynamics)
- **C** — [Wave behaviour](C-wave-behaviour/) (simple harmonic motion, waves, superposition, Doppler effect)
- **D** — [Fields](D-fields/) (gravitational fields, electric & magnetic fields, circuits, electromagnetic induction)
- **E** — [Nuclear and quantum physics](E-nuclear-and-quantum-physics/) (atomic, nuclear, quantum and particle physics)
