# Chemistry SL

IB syllabus: organised along two axes — Structure and Reactivity.

- **Structure 1** — [Models of the particulate nature of matter](structure-1-particulate-nature/) (atoms, electron configuration, periodicity)
- **Structure 2** — [Models of bonding and structure](structure-2-bonding-and-structure/) (ionic, covalent, metallic bonding)
- **Structure 3** — [Classification of matter](structure-3-classification/) (periodic table, organic chemistry, spectroscopy)
- **Reactivity 1** — [What drives chemical reactions?](reactivity-1-what-drives-reactions/) (energetics, enthalpy)
- **Reactivity 2** — [How much, how fast and how far?](reactivity-2-how-much-fast-far/) (stoichiometry, rates, equilibrium)
- **Reactivity 3** — [What are the mechanisms?](reactivity-3-mechanisms/) (acids & bases, redox, organic mechanisms)
